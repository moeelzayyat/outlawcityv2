# ART BIBLE — v1

## Direction
Clean low-poly city, three district palettes (Old Town brick, Civic concrete, Downtown slate — as built), the atmosphere preset (ClockTime 16.5, warm haze, bloom 0.4) as built. Police lights (red/blue alternating) as built. Cars: flat-shaded, strong silhouettes, one accent colour per tier. No gore, no weapons imagery in the icon.

## Signature visual
The **unbanked counter**: a large money figure above the HUD row that grows with each crime and turns red at 3★. It is the icon's hero element too.
